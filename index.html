Agar aap 16% ya usse zyada rate wale loan ki EMI bhar rahe hain, to har mahine hazaron rupaye zyada de rahe ho sakte hain. Balance transfer mein aap apna baaki loan kam rate wale lender par le jaate hain.

Transfer ka kharcha zaroor jodein

  • Poore tenure mein total interest bachta hai.
  • Kuch lenders top-up loan bhi dete hain.
  • Chahein to tenure adjust kar sakte hain.

Personal loan lene ki 7 badi galtiyan

Personal loan guide · 7 min · 2026

Personal loan kabhi kabhi zaroori hota hai. Lekin bahut log ye 7 galtiyan karte hain, jo baad mein mehngi padti hain.

1. Sirf apni bank ko check karna

Zyada tar log seedha apni hi bank jaate hain. Jab tak aap kam se kam 3 lenders ki offer side by side nahi dekhte, aapko pata nahi ki aapka rate achha hai ya nahi.

2. CIBIL score na dekhna

Apply karne se pehle apna score jaan lein. Score kam ho to pehle use sudharein, warna rate zyada milega ya loan reject ho sakta hai.

3. Processing fee ignore karna

Sirf interest rate mat dekhein. Processing fee, GST aur baaki charges jod kar poori laagat dekhein.

4. Zaroorat se zyada loan lena

Aapki saari EMIs milakar income ka 50% se zyada nahi honi chahiye.

5. Bahut lamba tenure

Zyada tenure ka matlab kam EMI, lekin total interest bahut zyada. Sabse chhota tenure chunein jo aap aaram se bhar sakein.

6. Kai banks mein ek saath apply karna

Har formal application par hard inquiry hoti hai, aur kai inquiries score girati hain. Pehle compare karein, phir sirf sabse achhe option mein apply karein.

7. Terms na padhna

Prepayment aur foreclosure charges, floating ya fixed rate, late payment penalty. Sign karne se pehle inhe dhyan se padhein.

Flexi loan kya hai, personal loan se kaise alag

Flexi loan guide · 6 min · 2026

₹5 Lakh ki limit mili. Jo chahein nikalein, jab chahein wapas karein. Interest sirf nikaale hue amount par lagta hai.

Personal loan mein poore ₹5 Lakh par interest lagta hai. Flexi mein ₹2 Lakh nikaale to sirf ₹2 Lakh par. Wapas karne par limit phir ₹5 Lakh ho jaati hai.

Fayde

  • Sirf use kiye amount par interest.
  • Emergency mein kabhi bhi withdraw.
  • Repay karte hi limit dobara available.
  • Kai lenders mein shuruaati period mein sirf interest dena hota hai.
  • Irregular income wale logon ke liye upyogi.

Dhyan rakhne wali baatein

  • Kuch lenders par flexi ka rate term loan se thoda zyada hota hai.
  • Fayda tab hai jab aap poori limit lambe time tak use nahi karte.
  • Withdrawal aur repayment ke terms, aur prepayment charges lender ke hisaab se alag hote hain. Sign karne se pehle padhein.
